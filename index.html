Renewables

The grant pays £7,500. We do the paperwork.

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme pays £7,500 towards an air source heat pump on eligible properties. We apply on your behalf and take it straight off your quote, so you never fund it yourself and claim it back.

Everything starts with a room-by-room heat loss survey, because that is what decides whether a heat pump will heat your home cheaply. Free, and yours to keep either way.

How we work

No salespeople. Ever.

  1. You speak to an engineer

    Not a call centre. You get a straight answer about whether it is something we do and what is involved.

  2. We survey, free of charge

    Heat loss room by room for heat pumps, gas rate and system condition for boilers. Measured, not guessed.

  3. One fixed written price

    Itemised, valid 30 days, with any grant already deducted. Extras only ever with your agreement first.

  4. The same engineer fits it

    On the agreed dates, property protected, cleared up each day, commissioned and handed over before we invoice.

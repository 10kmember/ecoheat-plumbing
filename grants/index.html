Grants & funding

The £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant explained

The government pays £7,500 towards an air source heat pump on eligible properties in England and Wales. We check whether you qualify, apply on your behalf, and take the grant off your quote.

What the grant is worth

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) is administered by Ofgem. It currently pays £7,500 towards an air source heat pump and the same amount towards a ground source heat pump. It is a grant, not a loan: there is nothing to repay.

Crucially, the money is claimed by the installer, not the householder. That means you never have to find £7,500 up front and wait to be reimbursed — it is deducted from your quotation, and you pay the balance.

Who is eligible

Your property normally qualifies if all of the following are true:

  • It is a domestic property in England or Wales.
  • It has a valid Energy Performance Certificate with no outstanding recommendations for loft or cavity wall insulation.
  • You are replacing a fossil fuel system — mains gas, oil, LPG or electric heating.
  • The property is not a new build (with limited exceptions for self-builds).
  • The installation is carried out to MCS standards by an MCS-certified installer.

We check your EPC and confirm eligibility in writing before you commit to anything. If you do not qualify, we will tell you at that point rather than after the survey.

How we handle the application

  1. Eligibility check. We look up your EPC and confirm the property qualifies.
  2. Heat loss survey. Room by room, free of charge, producing the design flow temperature and the emitter schedule.
  3. Quotation. Fixed price, itemised, with the £7,500 already deducted.
  4. Voucher application. We apply to Ofgem and you confirm the application by email — this is the only step that needs you.
  5. Installation and commissioning. Certified to MCS standards and registered on the MCS database.
  6. Redemption. We redeem the voucher. You have already had the benefit, so nothing further is due from you.

MCS accreditation and your grant

Our air source heat pump installations are designed, commissioned and certified with our MCS-accredited installation partner. That accreditation is what makes your installation eligible for the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant, and the certificate is issued in your name on completion. Day to day you deal with EcoHeat: we survey the property, carry out the work and look after it afterwards.

Other funding worth checking

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme is the main one, but depending on your circumstances you may also be eligible for:

  • ECO4 — support for households on qualifying benefits or in low-EPC properties, delivered through energy suppliers.
  • Great British Insulation Scheme — insulation measures that can also bring an EPC up to the standard the heat pump grant requires.
  • 0% VAT — heat pumps, solar thermal and related energy saving materials currently attract zero-rated VAT on installation in domestic properties.

If a grant will not cover it, finance is available on qualifying installations.

Grant rules change

Scheme values and eligibility criteria are set by government and can be revised. We confirm the current position in writing with your quotation. The authoritative source is Ofgem's Boiler Upgrade Scheme guidance.

Grant questions

How much is the heat pump grant and do I have to claim it back?

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme pays £7,500 towards an air source heat pump on eligible properties in England and Wales. You never pay it out and claim it back — we apply on your behalf and the grant is deducted from your quote, so you only ever pay the balance.

Which properties are eligible for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme?

Domestic properties in England and Wales with a valid EPC that has no outstanding loft or cavity wall insulation recommendations. The property must be replacing a fossil fuel system — gas, oil, LPG or electric — and new build properties are generally excluded. We check your EPC before quoting and tell you where you stand.

What does an air source heat pump cost to install?

The honest answer is that it depends on the heat loss of your property and how many radiators need upsizing, which is exactly what the survey establishes. We give you a fixed written price after the heat loss survey, with the £7,500 grant already deducted, and there is no charge for the survey.

Are you MCS certified?

Our air source heat pump installations are designed, commissioned and certified with our MCS-accredited installation partner. That accreditation is what makes your installation eligible for the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant, and the certificate is issued in your name on completion. Day to day you deal with EcoHeat: we survey the property, carry out the work and look after it afterwards.

Do heat pumps work in cold weather?

Yes. Air source heat pumps extract heat from outside air well below freezing, and they are the standard heating system in Scandinavia. Efficiency falls as it gets colder, which is why the system is sized against the coldest design temperature for the region rather than an average day.

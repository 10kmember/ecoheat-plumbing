About us

About EcoHeat Plumbing and Renewables

A small, independent Somerset firm doing plumbing, heating and renewables properly — with the same engineer from first phone call to final handover.

Who we are

EcoHeat Plumbing and Renewables Limited was incorporated in 2024 and works out of Brent House Farm at Edingworth, between Weston-super-Mare and Burnham-on-Sea. The company is new; the trade experience behind it is not.

We deliberately stayed small. There is no call centre, no sales team on commission and no target to shift a particular manufacturer's boiler. When you ring, you get an engineer. When you get a quote, it comes from the person who will be standing in your airing cupboard.

What we believe about heating

Two things, mainly. First, that most heating systems in Somerset are running badly rather than broken — wrong flow temperature, no balancing, an oversized boiler short-cycling itself to death. Fixing that is usually cheaper than replacing anything.

Second, that renewables only work when they are designed. A heat pump sized off a rule of thumb will be noisy, expensive and disappointing, and it will put the next three households off. We do the heat loss survey properly because the alternative damages the technology's reputation as well as the customer.

Credentials

We are Gas Safe registered under register number 952210, which you can verify at gassaferegister.co.uk. The company is registered in England and Wales, number 15532701, and we hold public liability insurance.

Our air source heat pump installations are designed, commissioned and certified with our MCS-accredited installation partner. That accreditation is what makes your installation eligible for the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant, and the certificate is issued in your name on completion. Day to day you deal with EcoHeat: we survey the property, carry out the work and look after it afterwards.

How we price

Surveys are free. Quotations are fixed, written and itemised, and valid for 30 days. On installations we take a deposit that covers materials, with the balance due on completion and commissioning — never before the system is finished and working. If we find something genuinely hidden once we start, we stop, explain it and re-quote rather than adding it to the invoice.

How we work

You deal with the engineer

No account managers, no handovers between departments. The person who surveys the job carries it through.

The price is the price

Fixed, written and itemised before we start. Extras only ever with your agreement, in writing, first.

We say no when it is right

If a repair is more sensible than a replacement, or your property is a poor candidate for a heat pump, you will hear that from us.

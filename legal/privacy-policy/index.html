Legal

Privacy policy

What we do with your personal data, why we are allowed to, how long we keep it and how to get it back or have it deleted.

Last updated 2026-08-16

Who we are

Ecoheat Plumbing and Renewables Limited (“EcoHeat”, “we”, “us”) is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. We are registered in England and Wales under company number 15532701, with a registered office at Suite 106, Viney Court, Viney Street, Taunton, Somerset, TA1 3FB and a trading address at Brent House Farm, Edingworth Road, Edingworth, Weston-super-Mare, BS24 0JA.

For anything to do with your personal data, contact us at info@ecoheatplumbingandrenewables.co.uk or on 01934 440290.

What personal data we collect

We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and carry out work you ask us to do:

  • Contact details — your name, telephone number, email address and the postcode or address of the property.
  • Enquiry details — what you tell us about the job, including any photographs you send us.
  • Job records — surveys, quotations, certificates, invoices and service history for work we carry out.
  • Correspondence — emails, WhatsApp messages and notes of telephone calls.

We do not collect special category data, and we do not ask for payment card details through this website.

We do not send marketing email or SMS to people who have simply made an enquiry, and we never sell or rent your details to anyone.

Who we share it with

Our website contact form opens your own email application and sends the message directly to us. It is not submitted to this website and no third-party form processor receives your details.

Beyond that, we share personal data only where it is necessary:

  • Gas Safe Register and Building Control — notification of notifiable gas work, as required by law.
  • Manufacturers — to register your warranty in your name.
  • Ofgem and MCS — where we apply for a Boiler Upgrade Scheme voucher on your behalf, and to register the installation.
  • Our MCS-accredited installation partner — where a heat pump installation is certified through them.
  • Finance providers — only if you ask us to arrange finance, and only with your knowledge.
  • Our accountant and IT providers — under confidentiality obligations.
  • Insurers and legal advisers — if a claim or dispute arises.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become jobs — deleted within 24 months.
  • Job and installation records — kept for the life of the installation plus 6 years, because gas safety records, warranty claims and liability all depend on them.
  • Accounting records — 6 years plus the current financial year, as required by HMRC.
  • Gas safety certificates — at least 2 years, and normally for the life of the appliance record.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • ask us to delete data we no longer need (this does not override records we must keep by law);
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it, such as for photographs of your installation;
  • ask for your data in a portable format.

Email info@ecoheatplumbingandrenewables.co.uk and we will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and does not profile visitors. See our cookie policy for the detail.

Security

Enquiries and job records are held in access-controlled accounts protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication where the provider supports it. This website is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to a business of our size.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes affecting existing customers will be notified directly.

Cookie policy

A short page, because there is very little to declare.

Last updated 2026-08-16

The short version

This website sets no cookies of its own, uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and does not profile you. There is nothing to consent to, which is why you are not being asked to dismiss a banner.

What that means in practice

  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar tracking.
  • No advertising or re-marketing cookies.
  • No third-party fonts, maps or embedded video loading from external servers on page load.
  • No cross-site tracking of any kind.

Your browser may store ordinary technical information such as its own cache. That is not something this site controls or can read.

Server logs

Our hosting provider keeps standard access logs — IP address, timestamp, page requested and browser type — for security and to keep the site running. These are ordinary server logs, not cookies, and they are not used to build a profile of you.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or any non-essential cookie, we will ask for your consent before it is set, and this page will be updated first. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, non-essential cookies require opt-in consent, and we will treat it that way.
